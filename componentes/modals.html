VR Simulator for Firefighters

Period: August 2023 - September 2024

Role: Developer and Principal Researcher

This project involved the development of an immersive virtual reality simulator aimed at training firefighters in emergency response involving hazardous materials, based on the content of the PRIMAP (First Response to Incidents with Hazardous Materials) course. The objective was to provide a safe and realistic training tool that would allow users to face critical situations and make key decisions under simulated high-pressure conditions. My involvement included both the pedagogical design and technical development of the simulator, integrating interactive scenarios that guide the user through different stages of the intervention procedure, with immediate feedback based on their choices. To this end, I worked closely with staff from the Technical Development Department of the Chilean National Fire Academy, which ensured the accuracy of the content. I also modeled and integrated 3D assets representative of industrial environments and elements of response equipment. The simulator was tested with firefighters from real firefighting companies in Talca and San Rafael, validating its usability and relevance as a complementary resource in professional training.

Blood System AR

Period: July 2025 - April 2026

Role: AR Developer

Blood Systems AR is an educational augmented reality application developed in Unity for Android and iOS, designed to support the learning of blood systems, cellular membranes, antigens, and protein structures through interactive 3D visualization. By scanning physical images, users can generate and manipulate biological models in augmented reality, transforming complex scientific concepts into immersive and interactive learning experiences.

A major focus of the project was the development of a modular and scalable architecture based on ScriptableObjects, allowing new educational content to be added without modifying core systems. One of the most challenging features was the implementation of a dynamic antigen network built on hexagonal grids, where users can solve puzzles by expanding molecular structures through drag-and-drop interactions. The application also includes membrane generation systems, comparison modes, undo functionality, real-time model scaling, and image-tracking capabilities using AR Foundation.

Beyond the AR experience itself, the project emphasized maintainability, reusability, and clean software architecture. Multiple independent systems—including antigens, membranes, UI, educational puzzles, and interaction mechanics—were designed to work together while remaining highly extensible, making the application suitable for future scientific content and educational expansions.

BubbleStress

Period: January 2025

Role: Lead Programmer

BubbleStress was developed during the Global Game Jam 2025, which had the theme "bubbles." The game offers a playful and sensorial experience in which the player takes on the role of a stressed office worker who must correctly type the letters that appear on the screen. The distinctive feature of the gameplay is that the keyboard simulates a pop-up, combining fast-typing mechanics with elements of relaxation and visual and audio satisfaction. As lead programmer, I was responsible for the design and implementation of the core game logic, including letter-pressed detection, real-time reaction mechanics, the scoring system, and the integration of visual and auditory feedback. I also optimized the overall game flow and collaborated on design iterations to ensure an intuitive and entertaining experience.

Space Shooter

Period: Nov 2025

Role: Lead Programmer

This project involved the development of a 2D Space Shooter in Unity focused on modular and scalable game systems. The game features progressive difficulty, collectible power-ups, enemy waves, and a complete upgrade shop where players can improve their abilities using in-game currency.

Pixel Hero

Period: Dec 2025

Role: Lead Programmer

Pixel Hero is a Top-Down action-adventure game developed in Unity that combines exploration, combat, and progression systems. Players can explore interconnected levels, battle enemies, interact with NPCs, collect items, and manage resources through an inventory system.

A key feature of the project is the enemy AI, implemented using a finite state machine with patrol, chase, attack, and idle behaviors. The game also includes ScriptableObject-based inventories, consumable items, dialogue systems, and scene transitions, all developed using a modular object-oriented architecture.

Elemental Change

Period: March 2022 - July 2022

Role: Lead Programmer

Elemental Change is an action-adventure platform game in which the player takes control of Shin, a being created to restore balance to the world, facing challenges in a world divided between the elements of Water, Earth, Fire, and Air. Through platforming and skill mechanics, Shin must overcome obstacles, unlock elemental abilities, and unleash elemental spirits to restore balance to the world.

As Lead Programmer, I was responsible for implementing the game's core programming, which included platforming mechanics, character abilities, and enemy AI. I developed the skill system, which allowed Shin to use elemental powers to interact with the environment and confront enemies. I was also in charge of save point management and puzzle integration, ensuring smooth transitions between levels and a consistent gameplay experience with the narrative. I worked closely with the artists and designers to ensure the game mechanics were properly integrated with the visual assets.
